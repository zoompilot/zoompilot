# Offroad-transition forensics, routes 208 .. 211 (device logs, 2026-09-10)

Read-only analysis of the rlogs on the test device. All routes ran commit 309f10f708 (branch
`jetson-trt`, opendbc fcdbf158), MAZDA_CX5_2022, `openpilotLongitudinalControl=True` (safetyParam 3)
except 210/211 (alpha long off, safetyParam 2). Route time = seconds since the first message of
segment 0. Sources: `can` bus 0 (src0) decoded from mazda_2017.dbc, `sendcan` (openpilot TX),
`pandaStates`, `deviceState`, `logMessage` (cloudlog/carlog), `carState`/`carControl`.
Tables: `route-<id>-events.txt` (from `route_forensics.py`), TX attribution: `sendcan_census.py`.

Notation: PEDALS tuple = (ACC_OFF=armed, ACC_ACTIVE, BRAKE_ON, STANDSTILL).
CRZ_INFO tuple = (ACC_ACTIVE, ACC_SET_ALLOWED, NEW_SIGNAL_7). CRZ_CTRL tuple = (AVAIL, ACTIVE, GAP, ACTIVE_2).
`src128/130` = frames the panda itself put on bus 0/2 (its forwarded copies AND openpilot's TX);
openpilot's own TX is attributed from `sendcan` only. `src192/194` = panda rejection reports.

## Segments present on the device

208 (seg 0), 209 (0-1), 20a (0), 20b (0-25), 20c (0-9), 20d (0-1), 20e (0-3), 20f (0-23),
210 (0-7), 211 (0-1). All have rlog.zst. Nothing in this range was deleted.

Only 20d->20e is a device reboot. 208->209, 209->20a and 20c->20d are software stops with
ignition on and the panda running throughout (uptime continuous). The OffroadMode signature:
pandad sets `power_save_desired = !ignition_local` with `ignition_local = ign && !always_offroad`,
so `powerSaveEnabled=1` while `ignitionCan=1` can only come from `OffroadMode` being set. An onroad
cycle (`OnroadCycleRequested`) only closes the relay (noOutput) and never enables power save.

Origin: the stop was brokered by hardwared (`HardwaredExt.update`), i.e. `OnroadCycleRequested`
or `OffroadModeRequested`. Power save with ignition CAN still high proves `OffroadMode` was written,
which only the `OffroadModeRequested` branch does: **the UI's Force Offroad (`set_always_offroad`)**.
Not the alpha toggle (carParams opLong stayed True in 20c and 20d; that path also needs
standstill and logs `mazdaRadarSession ... requested` only), not a reboot (no `DoReboot`
line, panda uptime continuous into 20d), not an ignition drop. 20d began 34 s later at 113.6 km/h
when Force Offroad was exited (OffroadMode cleared) while moving.
The UI logs nothing on the button press itself; the attribution rests on the power-save signature.
The hand-back itself worked: default-session ack 0.34 s after the request, stock traffic back
0.5 s after, cycle answered 0.9 s after the request, at ~32.7 m/s.

Stock CRZ_CTRL payloads after the hand-back (569.65-570.07): `0000000000000000` and
`0360000000000000` only, i.e. the radar restarted its boot sequence (same payloads as the first
0.4 s of 20f and 20c). The route ended 0.4 s later so nothing more is visible in 20c. In 20d (34 s
later) the stock stream is normal (`0102 81 40..`, `0102 87 40..`, `0902 87..` while engaged).

Gate: `RadarSessionManager.update` leaves STOCK only on `gate_passed and bus_healthy` with either
`stock_radar_gone` (radar silent 1.27 s on a healthy bus) or `standstill and stock_radar_alive`
("parked takeover"). `gate_passed = fsc_settled and not (stock_radar_alive and cruise_enabled)`.
The stock radar was alive the whole route and the car never stood still, so the parked-takeover
branch was never reachable; additionally stock MRCC was engaged 15.8-23.9, 61.5-66.2 and
93.6-99.6 s, which blocks `gate_passed`. Availability is masked (`available = cruise_available and
silenced`) until a takeover, so the driver's MAIN/SET presses armed and engaged stock MRCC
(panda controlsAllowed went True) but openpilot showed cruise unavailable. No CRZ_BTNS was
transmitted by openpilot in 20d or 20e (sendcan census: zero 0x9d), so every press is the wheel's.
20c->20d is Force Offroad entry (20c 569.1 s) and exit (20d 0 s) while moving; the reboot at
115.4 s came from the UI (`DoReboot`); the manager's gate answered within 0.5 s because there was
nothing to hand back (`handback_completed` is set after 10 stock frames when nothing was silenced).

Answer: the driver did not press MAIN after the takeover. The last cruise button of the route is
the CAN_OFF at 77.488 s, 60 s before the takeover; the body's ACC_OFF was 0 from 77.559 s to the
end. Before the takeover the body answered every MAIN press (six armings, one stock MRCC
engagement), so the body ECU was healthy, and under a completed takeover the body also answers
MAIN in every other route on the device (20c 67.9 s, 20f 110.1 s and 168.5 s, 20a 14.5 s: PEDALS
ACC_OFF rises 50-80 ms after the press, `cruiseState.available` follows 2 ms later, and
openpilot's CRZ_CTRL flips AVAIL to 1 on the next 50 Hz frame). The software derivation was
therefore correct on both sides: `available` was masked while the stock radar owned the bus
(by design), and after the takeover `cruise_available` was False because PEDALS said so.
openpilot's replacement CRZ_CTRL carried AVAIL=0 and CRZ_INFO (0,0,0) for the whole silenced
phase, which mirrors the body's "main off"; this is the correct idle advertisement, not a cause.

What is not established: why the driver stopped pressing MAIN. The plausible reading from
20d/20e is that after the reboot at speed the presses at 12-77 s armed/engaged stock MRCC while
openpilot showed cruise unavailable (masked until the first standstill), the driver gave up, and
the takeover at 137 s then went unused. There is no UI log to confirm what was displayed.

## Degraded-radar signature check (fault CRZ_CTRL `1a 01 01 00 02 00 06 00`, PEDALS ACC_OFF cycling)

Not present anywhere in 208-211. Distinct stock (src0) CRZ_CTRL payloads seen:
- boot: `0360000000000000`, `0361010000000000`, `0261010000000000` (first ~5.8 s of 20c, 20f; 20b/20a similar)
- idle main off: `0201010000000000`, `0102814000000000`, `0102816000000000`, `0201816000000000`, `0201812000000000`, `0201818000000000`, `020181a000000000`, `0102816a00000000`
- armed: `0102874000000000`, `0102872000000000`, `0201070000000000`, `0102878000000000`; cancel transitions carry byte 4 = 0x38 for ~0.2 s (`0102874000380000`, `0102872000380000`, `0102812000380000`, `0102878000380000`, `0102818000380000`, `0102816000380000`)
- engaged: `0902874000000000`, `0902874000001000`, `0902878000001000`, `0902872000001000`, `0a01070000001000` (openpilot's own engaged payload, src128 only)
- after the 20c hand-back: `0000000000000000`, `0360000000000000` (radar re-init) for the 0.4 s before the route ended.
Every PEDALS ACC_OFF edge in 20c-20f sits within 80 ms after a wheel MAIN/CAN_OFF/SET press
or a SET engagement (listed in the tables); there is no unprompted ACC_OFF cycling.

## Camera / EPS bits

No STEER_RATE.LKAS_FAULT, CAM_LKAS.ERR_BIT_1, CAM_LANEINFO.ERR_BIT, steerFaultPermanent or
accFaulted edge to 1 in 20b-20f. The only CAM_LANEINFO bits are the normal boot pair
NO_ERR_BIT=1,BIT2=1 for ~5.7 s after a car ignition on (20b, 20c, 20f), which is exactly what
`fsc_settled` waits out before a takeover (FSC_SETTLE_T=10 s after it clears: takeovers at
13.6/15.9/15.8 s).
